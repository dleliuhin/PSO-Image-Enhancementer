# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and focused on the technical work.
Accept feedback gracefully, give credit, and assume good intent while still
addressing harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
trolling, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `dleliuhin@mail.ru`. Maintainers may
remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently exclude
participants whose behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
