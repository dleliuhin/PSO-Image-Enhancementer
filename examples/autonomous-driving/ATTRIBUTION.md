# Autonomous-driving sample attribution

The source frames in this directory are adapted from the
[CARLA–nuScenes dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/collabora/carla-nuscenes),
created by Collabora and distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source subset: `samples/CAM_FRONT/batch_22.zip`.

The files were resized and, for `uneven-light-input.jpg`, deterministically
darkened with a spatially varying illumination mask to provide a controlled
reference-image experiment. These image assets and their enhanced derivatives
remain available under CC BY 4.0 and are not covered by the repository's MIT
software license.

No endorsement by Collabora, CARLA, or the nuScenes project is implied.
